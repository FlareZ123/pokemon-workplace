**Shadow Rider versus Iron Thorns continuation:** The [exact Cologne/Flutter source partition](tests/shadow_rider_thorns_continuation/) decomposes the existing 7.327398814% restricted K1-style opening-launch access into 3.710015146% Cologne-only, 3.199759737% Flutter-only, and 0.417623931% reachable through either source. [Windows CI passed](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38728582546). The other-Trainer cuts are not chosen, and their tactical opportunity costs, turn-two energy/lock continuation, and actual match effects are untested. No winning-deck recommendation follows from this access improvement alone.

## How to interpret strength

The research contains exact mathematical probabilities, constrained first-turn launch simulations, card-rule regressions, physical conservation tests, and tournament observations. These answer different questions. **A first-turn access probability is not a match win rate.** Candidate swaps that improve an access endpoint can still worsen tactical value under lock effects, Supporter contention, occupied Bench slots, Prize configurations, resource discard requirements, or other metagames.

Current priority is reproducible full-game headless match evaluation between the tournament baselines, including realistic opposing policies and verified card-engine coverage. Until that evidence exists, prioritize decks by credible tournament results, tactical matchup considerations, and the rules-validity of their key lines. The repository [Twinleaf simulation integration](../simulation/twinleaf/) uses a pinned upstream submodule at [`vendor/twinleafgg`](../vendor/twinleafgg/); automated engine support must be checked card by card before interpreting match results.

**Observation-blind search-Item diagnostic:** [Pinned Pokégear/Trainers' Mail K0 policy](tests/twinleaf_blind_topdeck_search/) replaces two secret-topdeck-dependent native Item choices with a common hypergeometric decision across indistinguishable hidden worlds. The [isolated SFT passed](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38607914259); an opt-in `blind_topdeck` bounded-runner mode leaves all other native information leaks flagged. **This is a policy-regression result, not match-strength evidence.**

**Experimental K0 hidden-world policy boundary:** [Canonical sampled private-game states](tests/twinleaf_k0_hidden_world_sampler/) establish a state-cloning and physical-conservation invariant for the pinned Twinleaf bot. The [isolated 60-card-world regression passed](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38609765476). A separate `sampled_k0` bounded diagnostic mode is explicitly **single-sample, open-opponent-decklist and history-unaware**, with prompt-free sampling only. Outcomes cannot be treated as competitive win rates.

## Technical research and replication

- **[Verified evolution parents in current paper Expanded](verified_evolution_parent_semantics/):** official card identity checks repair missing Zoroark `evolvesFrom: Zorua` and misspelled Antique Plume Fossil parents on two White Flare Archen prints. All three are included in the reproducible 75-print Python/Node [source-normalization manifest](verified_card_metadata_boundary/), with exact-print mutation guards and Windows regression. These are card-data corrections; deck match effects remain untested.

- **[Wobbuffet versus Flutter Mane under Iron Thorns](wobbuffet_flutter_thorns_continuation/):** exact-print lock graph distinguishes Psychic Wobbuffet's suppression of Iron Thorns and Radiant Eternatus from Flutter Mane's suppression of Thorns alone. Wobbuffet restores established Psychic Underworld Door but cannot simultaneously enable Radiant Eternatus's Dragon-type launch while Active. Stealthy Hood and Jamming Tower are tested. Conditional rules result, not match evidence.

- **[Dream Ball / Alolan Muk exact opening-Prize allocation](dream_ball_muk_opening_allocation/):** an exact static opening calculation for a hypothetical 14-Basic, singleton-Muk, one-to-four-Dream-Ball deck. With one Dream Ball among the first uniformly awarded Prize, the conditional Basic-valid event is 1.344856%; three tiny decks confirm the formula by exhaustive labeled enumeration. This is an opening allocation prerequisite, not an achievable gameplay rate or match-win estimate.

- **[Alolan Muk versus Iron Thorns ex source precedence](ability_lock_muk_thorns_precedence/):** exact-print shared Ability-lock graph regression shows Bench Muk suppresses Active Thorns Initialization, restoring Rule-Box Evolution Abilities but suppressing Basic support. Stealthy Hood and Jamming Tower change the dependency. Conditional mechanics only, not match-win evidence.

- **[Twinleaf hidden-Prize Quick Ball oracle](tests/twinleaf_hidden_prize_search_oracle/):** exact 52-world Quick Ball plus 104-world Ultra Ball/Mysterious Treasure K0 counterexamples with pinned-native-bot regressions demonstrating Prized Basic foresight. [Manual CI passed](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38608719207); engine match outcomes remain unfit for competitive ranking.
- **[Observation-consistent K0 paid-search policy](tests/twinleaf_blind_paid_search_policy/):** separately selectable first-order expected Item decision heuristic for three paid searches; all 156 hidden-world action invariance tests and one two-seat bounded engine smoke test passed. Stronger K1 tracking and whole-bot audits remain open.
- **[Competitive deck baselines](competitive_deck_baselines/):** primary tournament transcription, full 60-card print lists, reproducible legality and key-text checks.
- **[Aichi Vileplume ALS](aichi_vileplume_als/):** constrained turn-one setup and timing analysis. The model's Item-lock endpoint is not a game outcome.
- **[Mew/Genesect VIP4 opening analysis](mew_genesect_vip_opening/):** exact early Pokémon-access comparisons for source-aware paid search.
- **[Regidrago K0 launch](regidrago_k0_joint_launch/):** information-constrained opening sequence with explicit discard costs.
- **[Mega Absol lock-state lattice](absol_garbodor_lock_lattice/):** source-access changes under Item and Ability locks.
- **[Prize-to-Bench E-31 execution](prize_before_hand_bench_entry/):** official-ruling-backed KO Bench geometry, recursive Jirachi/Chansey Prize chain and Billowing Smoke hand-destination gate; verified by [CI run 38291234939](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38291234939).
- **[Raichu physical Item payment](tests/raichu_target_payment_frontier/):** exact one-turn Alolan Raichu search through Ultra Ball/Computer Search with six hidden Prizes, Gladion fallback and mutually competing two-card discard costs; restricted access **30.578700%** under strict payment and **33.390969%** under an Ultra-for-Computer-sacrifice policy. [Migrated Windows CI 38292324506 passed](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38292324506); this is card access, not wins.
- **[Marshadow Path counter correction](tests/shadow_rider_marshadow_path/):** the champion's existing non-Rule-Box Marshadow supplies a Bench-based Resetting Hole answer omitted in earlier restricted models. Adding it raises the one-Cologne/one-Flutter Energy-only conditional first-turn launch from 2.027751% to 2.811878% and changes the three-slot ordering; exact Windows [regression passed](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38400970326). No match wins established.
- **[Shadow Rider Fog Crystal-to-Marshadow correction](tests/shadow_rider_fog_marshadow/):** four existing Fog Crystal add a missing legal search route to the champion's Marshadow Path answer. For one Cologne/one Flutter, the restricted Path + Iron Thorns first-turn launch endpoint rises from **2.811878% to 5.039163%** under an Energy-only Mysterious Treasure discard policy; all 15 exact 60-card scenarios and a 135,072-world physical oracle are reproduced. This remains conditional access, not match strength.
- **[Shadow Rider shared Fog Crystal allocation](tests/shadow_rider_fog_allocation/):** exact physical allocation of four Fog Crystal between Marshadow and Shadow Rider V for Forest Seal Stone, avoiding connector double-counting. The one-Cologne/one-Flutter restricted Path + Thorns access increases further **5.039163% to 5.426284%**; 15 exact scenarios, 247,590-world toy oracle, [Windows CI passed](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38610852773). Conditional access only.
- **[Shadow Rider paid search allocation](tests/shadow_rider_paid_search_allocation/):** full-60-card exact one-Cologne/one-Flutter Path + Iron Thorns opening-access calculation adds paid Mysterious Treasure / Quick Ball searches and Fog Crystal funding of Psychic Energy. Restricted feasibility increases **5.426284% → 7.327399%**, with an 83,720-world physical oracle and [passing Windows CI](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38611969086). **Information-privileged K1-style feasibility upper bound before first deck search**, not an achievable K0 policy or match win rate.
- **[Shadow Rider Treasure payment sensitivity](tests/shadow_rider_treasure_payment/):** exact Path + Iron Thorns counter-launch access falls from 2.622360% to 2.027751% with an Energy-only Mysterious Treasure discard policy. The source-tested endpoints are conditional first-turn access, not match outcomes.
- **[First-action information value](first_action_information_value/):** exact information value under hidden-Prize decisions, with restricted Regidrago cases.
- **[Two-player identity conservation](match_scoped_identity_liveness/):** reusable exact physical-card invariant for joint match modeling.
- **[Partial Prize observability](gnh_partial_prize_information/):** narrow decision-dependent value of Prized card knowledge.

## Research decisions still open

1. Measure realistic matches and matchup spreads across several independently validated strategies, with complete decklists, fixed bot policy and simulator revision.
2. Audit key attack-copying, lock interaction, Prize replacement and Trainer payment mechanics before trusting automated-game conclusions.
3. Retest individual card substitutions with opponent-dependent utility, unknown-Prize information, and multi-turn continuation costs.
4. Curate or retire superseded tests as evidence accumulates, retaining enough detail to reproduce each substantive finding.
