# Reacquisition ledger -> exact Trainer transaction bridge

## Question

The temporal resource ledger shows that a required payload can become temporarily discardable when a later search reacquires another copy.

Does that three-filler witness survive when the deck, hand, discard pile, Supporter quota, and exact search targets are conserved by the canonical Trainer transaction layer?

Yes.

This result composes:

- `temporal_resource_ledger.py`;
- `trainer_search_transaction.py`;
- the typed Trainer search allocator;
- exact discard witnesses.

Regression: `results/reacquisition_transaction_bridge/reproduce.py`

## Ledger prediction

The abstract sequence is:

1. Secret Box discards three initial fillers and generates Guzma & Hala, Tag Call, TM: Evolution, and Artazon.
2. Guzma & Hala discards two cards and generates a replacement TM: Evolution plus Jet Energy.

The final hand must contain:

- TM: Evolution;
- Artazon;
- Jet Energy.

The provenance-aware ledger reports a minimum of three initial fillers.

Its second discard witness uses:

- Tag Call;
- the TM: Evolution generated by Secret Box.

The later action recreates TM: Evolution before the final requirement is checked.

## Exact conserved execution

The exact transaction state begins with:

- Secret Box in hand;
- exactly three filler classes in hand;
- Tag Call, Guzma & Hala, and Artazon in deck;
- two copies of TM: Evolution in deck;
- one Jet Energy in deck.

### Secret Box

The exact three-card discard witness consumes all three initial fillers.

Secret Box retrieves:

- Tag Call;
- one TM: Evolution;
- Guzma & Hala;
- Artazon.

One TM: Evolution remains in deck.

### Guzma & Hala

The retrieved Guzma & Hala is played from that exact hand.

Its two-card discard witness consumes:

- Tag Call;
- the first TM: Evolution.

The paid branch retrieves:

- the second TM: Evolution;
- Jet Energy.

It deliberately takes no Stadium because Artazon is already retained in hand.

The final hand contains exactly the required payload classes:

- one TM: Evolution;
- one Artazon;
- one Jet Energy.

The Supporter quota is consumed only by Guzma & Hala.

Per-card-class totals are conserved across the full two-action sequence.

## Result

The abstract ledger's three-filler optimum is mechanically realizable in the canonical zone model.

This closes the main limitation noted by `reacquisition_discardability/` for this witness: the replacement TM is not declared from nowhere. It is the second physical class copy remaining in the deck after Secret Box retrieves the first.

## Strategic interpretation

The important distinction is between a required card class and a required current copy.

TM: Evolution remains required by the endpoint.

The first retrieved copy can still be strategically expendable because the second copy is reachable through the next legal action before the endpoint deadline.

This gives a concrete DCI transition:

- required while replacement access is absent;
- temporarily expendable while the replacement route is live;
- required again after the route has resolved.

## Limits

The regression is one deterministic mechanically valid witness.

It does not claim that discarding and reacquiring TM: Evolution is always strategically optimal, or that every Aichi opening supports the route.

The separate `reacquisition_discardability/` probe estimates how often sufficient raw copy availability exists under its stated conditioning.

## Next useful work

The next integration step is to let a turn planner discover this replacement route from conserved deck state automatically.

That requires the discard policy to look ahead through legal typed search actions and recognize that a current payload copy can be replaced before its deadline.
