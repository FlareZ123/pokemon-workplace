## Evidence and evaluation gap

A leading launch line is **Tag Call → Guzma & Hala → Jet Energy + TM: Evolution on Ω Barrage Bunnelby**, enabling two Evolution attacks going second and early Item-lock Vileplume. Active Stoutland imposes a distinct Supporter lock. Pidgeot ex enables sustained search. [Aichi ALS analysis](../../aichi_vileplume_als/) reports 36.3388% modeled early Item-lock setup over 500,000 valid openers under its specified policy, **not** a match win rate. [Guzma & Hala payment](../../aichi_gnh_discard_frontier/) and [Ability-lock precedence](../../ability_lock_established_precedence/) remain critical. There is no validated complete-game matchup win percentage yet.
