## Strategic assessment

Mew VMAX's Cross Fusion Strike and the Genesect V Fusion Strike System draw engine make this a fast Item-heavy attacker; a fourth VIP Pass is an attractive opening consistency hypothesis. However, Item locks and Ability locks can invalidate large parts of this access network. Published fifth-place tournament evidence supports competitive viability, without establishing present-day varied-field win rates.

An [exact first-turn Battle VIP experiment](../../mew_genesect_vip_opening/) reports first-turn Mew V + two Genesect V material access **32.256881%** for the published three-VIP list versus **40.058394%** for a legal four-VIP version removing the second Special Red Card, under the deliberately constrained natural-VIP access model. Later tests add Trainers' Mail and physically paid Ball routes. This is not a full competitive-utility ranking; the removed Special Red Card may decide actual games. Avoid counting these access figures as win rates.
