**Evidence standard:** the tournament finish is evidence of viability in the named event. Source-verified card mechanics and exact or sampled setup-access endpoints provide narrower supporting evidence. At present the repository has no sufficiently validated complete-match simulations comparing these candidates against a representative Expanded opponent field, so no field-wide win-rate ranking is asserted.

## Adding a deck

Keep a playable **60-card composition**, choose and check eligible English print IDs, make exact card counts explicit, state which source or transformation produced the list, and link the corresponding legality proof. Separate the reason to play it from the model assumptions, unresolved matchups, and any future costs sacrificed by the proposed swaps. Prefer adding a distinct variant only when its 60 cards or strategic role genuinely differ from an existing entry.

Narrow mechanical regressions and action probability studies belong under [the staged tests archive](../tests/), with historical links preserved during migration. Do not create duplicate folder names for the same 60-card list.
