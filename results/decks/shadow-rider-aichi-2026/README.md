## Why this deck is a leading candidate

Underworld Door accelerates Psychic Energy while drawing. Max Geist converts broad board Energy into damage, and Radiant Eternatus supplies a VMAX launch line. Copycat Mimikyu and Dialga-GX provide specific Regidrago counterplay. All roles and print legality are checked in [competitive_deck_baselines](../../competitive_deck_baselines/README.md).

**What has been tested:** [physical dual-entry launch](../../shadow_rider_dual_entry_launch/), [early Radiant line](../../shadow_rider_radiant_launch/), [Regidrago temporal counterplay](../../regidrago_shadow_rider_temporal_counterplay/), and [Iron Thorns counter](../../shadow_rider_cologne_thorns_counter/). These are bounded card-access or state-transition results and do not measure the winning probability of complete games.

**Priority:** test real matches against Vileplume control, Regidrago, Iron Thorns, Mew/Genesect and other representative Expanded archetypes with pinned simulator revision and validated card-effect coverage.
