## Evidence and risk

The [exact first-turn access evaluation](../../mew_genesect_vip_opening/) identifies a gain in naturally held Mew V plus two Genesect V materials from **32.256881%** (original three VIP) to **40.058394%** (this list, four VIP) on a valid seven-card starting hand, six Prize cards and going-second first natural draw. It models all naturally held VIP Passes and unprized target availability. Adding naturally held Trainers' Mail raised the corresponding source-access estimates further in its own model. Those percentages concern a single source-constrained opening event, and do not model full Fusion Strike System draws, opponent interaction, or later card value.

**Potential drawback:** cutting a Special Red Card reduces disruption density when the opponent has three or fewer Prizes left. First evaluate against plausible lock and late-game comeback matchups before preferring this list to the published baseline. Neither variant has verified matchup win rates.
