# Twinleaf paired seeds

Two reversed-seat tournament probes now share a seed and record canonical deck identifiers. See `simulation/twinleaf/paired-seat-plan.cjs` and `simulation/twinleaf/test-paired-seat-plan.cjs`.

[Standalone CI validated](https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38276376401).

Matching PRNG initial states do not prove equal opening deals, legal card behavior, or competitive win probabilities. Twinleaf's current policy is unvalidated.
