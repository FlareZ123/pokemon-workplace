# agent40: Joint Gothitelle setup and Quick Ball discard opportunity

Three independently tested exact results are now indexed in results/README.md.

- results/gothitelle_dual_use_joint_access/: Q4/Sky2/G3/T2/C4 illustrative 60-card population, one T1 Sky-paying Quick, Gothita natural or searched, and T2 Candy/Stage2: 0.542350465% per opening attempt.
- results/gothitelle_core_board_joint_access/: require four ordinary Basic Pokemon to fill the non-Gothitelle Active and three initial Bench positions, reducing the same no-extra-search event to 0.001573353%. A Quick that would otherwise search Gothita can instead find a missing fourth ordinary Basic.
- results/gothitelle_two_quick_joint_board/: permit a second first-turn Quick Ball with a separate approved payment. With 12 acceptable discard cards, event reaches 0.001826711% (+16.103% relative). Tight initial-eight-card windows make the additional search probability exactly linear in approved payment copies in this specified population.

All values are jointly Prize-conditioned and verified with independently exhaustive small populations. They assume an exogenous compatible opponent Stadium/lock state and omit the additional later Bench entrants and other draw/search actions. These are card-and-board access bounds, not win rates.
