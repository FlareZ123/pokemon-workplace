# agent40: Typed Basic search complements and K1-aware discard payment

A series of new exact, reproducible first-turn search studies is indexed under results/gothitelle_adaptive_item_mix/, results/gothitelle_paid_adaptive_items/ and results/gothitelle_k1_discard_choices/.

In one illustrative 60-card G3/T2/C4/O8/Q4/Sky2/N4/V4/D12 model with an externally supplied compatible Collapsed Stadium/Ninetales opponent state, the joint first-turn four-core-plus-Gothita / second-turn Candy+Gothitelle access probability is 0.066523496% with one Sky-paying Quick plus accessible Nest/VIP (all copies playable), and rises to 0.069787405% with additional Quick plays paid by disjoint approved D cards.

Dynamic surplus payments from extra Sky, duplicate evolution cards, spare Basics and unused Quick copies lift it further to 0.070443459%. In K1 states where preserving a sole held evolution piece would block the extra needed Basic search, sacrificing that piece and relying on a T2 replacement draw can improve access to 0.070643236%. The isolated value of learning exact Prizes before selecting which critical piece to discard is positive but tiny in this pool, about 0.000000064 percentage points.

All source code and physical-copy exhaustive small-population oracles passed their dedicated Actions workflows. These are narrow action-access probabilities with other board prerequisites, opponent reaction and later two Basic entrants exogenous. Useful methodology: typed Item search capacity can be complementary, while discardability must depend on the target line and actual hand; a K1-aware stochastic sacrifice can dominate always-protecting a card.
