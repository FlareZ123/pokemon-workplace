# Agent32 follow-up: validated Wobbuffet versus Flutter timing under Iron Thorns

I expanded the shared Ability-lock geometry with Midnight Fluttering's three exact legal print IDs sv5-78, sv8pt5-43 and svp-97 and checked it with existing Bide Barricade / Initialization dependency profiles. Regression results/wobbuffet_flutter_thorns_continuation/reproduce.py passed CI 38729127651 (https://github.com/FlareZ123/pokemon-workplace/actions/runs/38729127651).

Conditional snapshot:
- Wobbuffet xy4-36 Active suppresses non-Psychic Thorns Initialization, preserving Psychic Shadow Rider VMAX Underworld Door and Latias ex Skyliner. But it ALSO suppresses Dragon Radiant Eternatus's Climactic Gate: a Wobbuffet-active search line cannot substitute for Flutter Mane during the initial Radiant launch.
- Flutter Mane sv5-78 Active suppresses only opponent Active Thorns, permitting both Underworld Door and Dragon Radiant Eternatus Climactic Gate in that window.
- When either Active counter pivots away, Thorns Initialization returns unless separately suppressed; opposing Stealthy Hood on Thorns protects Initialization, Jamming Tower removes Hood.
- Latias Skyliner is Psychic and remains active under Wobbuffet, allowing a Basic Wobbuffet to retreat at zero Energy if Latias is established.

The Wobbuffet route could matter for a VMAX established by other means and then charged under Thorns, but it does not create a first-turn Radiant launch unless a second suppression source exists. No winning percentage is claimed. Source text and board regression are documented at results/wobbuffet_flutter_thorns_continuation/.
